# Dil Wali Diwali – Event Website

**A Celebration of Lights, Laughter & Togetherness**

This is a mobile-friendly static website created as part of the BBA Semester V  
**EventTech & Quality Management** digital system project.

## Event Details
- **Name:** Dil Wali Diwali
- **Date:** 7 November 2026
- **Duration:** 75 minutes
- **Venue:** College Classroom
- **Budget:** ₹700

## Website Sections
1. **Home** – Event name, tagline, date and festive visual identity
2. **About the Event** – Purpose, concept, objectives and theme
3. **Activities** – Human Bingo, Emoji Pictionary, Balloon Blast, Pour in the Bangle, Music & Dance, Diya celebration
4. **Agenda** – 75-minute programme schedule
5. **Venue** – College classroom details
6. **Registration** – Demo RSVP form (replace with live Google Form link for production)
7. **Contact** – Organising team contact placeholder

## How to Use
1. Open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. Or host the folder on any static hosting service (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, Google Sites, etc.).
3. For live registration, replace the demo form with your Google Form embed or link.

## Tech Stack
- HTML5
- CSS3 (responsive, festive Diwali colour palette)
- Vanilla JavaScript (mobile menu, form demo, active nav)

## Colours Used
- Orange – Energy & celebration
- Yellow / Gold – Light & happiness
- Pink – Joy & creativity
- Deep Purple – Festive contrast

## Notes for Project Demonstration
- The RSVP form is a **demo only**. Submissions are not stored online.
- Sample guest database, QR check-in and analytics are demonstrated separately via Google Sheets as described in the project document.
- Update the Contact section with real team details once confirmed.
