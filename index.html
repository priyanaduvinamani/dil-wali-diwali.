About the Event

Lights, Laughter & Togetherness

🎯

Concept

Dil Wali Diwali is a low-budget Pre-Diwali celebration planned for a college classroom. Students come together through interactive games, music, dance and simple festive activities.

🌟

Theme

Traditional Meets Modern — Traditional Diwali elements such as diyas and festive colours combined with contemporary classroom games, Bollywood music and dance.

👥

Audience

Primary audience is students from the organising class. Faculty members may be invited as guests. Expected capacity will be confirmed based on classroom size.

Event Objectives

  • Celebrate the festive spirit of Diwali in a fun and inclusive classroom setting.
  • Encourage interaction, teamwork and participation through four group and individual games.
  • Provide an enjoyable music and dance experience for students.
  • Plan and conduct a 75-minute event within the approved budget of ₹700.
  • Demonstrate event planning, registration, guest management and quality measurement as part of the BBA project.
